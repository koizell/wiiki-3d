## Extractos literales sobre rasgos físicos

> **Short verdict:** The Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra is an easy-to-recommend compact resin
> printer for miniatures, detailed models, and small prototype batches. Its 18-micron
> pixels, automatic leveling, tilting vat, useful self-checks, and Wi-Fi workflow
> remove real friction. [...]

> ## What the Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra is
> The Mars 5 Ultra is a **compact masked-resin printer** built around a **7-inch 9K
> monochrome LCD** and a 153.36 x 77.76 x 165 mm build volume. It is the
> feature-rich member of the Mars 5 pair: the Ultra adds the finer screen, a
> **tilting resin vat**, an **onboard camera**, Wi-Fi transfer, and a higher
> advertised speed ceiling.

> The tilting vat peels each cured layer from the release film by **changing the vat
> angle** instead of relying only on a long vertical lift. That can shorten layer
> cycles and reduce peel forces, but resin, geometry, supports, temperature, and
> settings still decide whether a job succeeds.

> The camera **sits low and looks through a dark cover**. 3DWithUs found the view
> adequate for basic monitoring and time-lapse with extra lighting, while Maker Hacks
> reported that the camera failed to identify multiple print failures. Treat it as a
> convenience camera, not a control system.

> The two-level build-plate design can trap resin around its structure, and the
> tilting vat needs space to move. Removing a wet plate can drip into the gap around
> the vat, **which is why Elegoo includes a drip tray**.

> The printer does not include a complete wash-and-cure workflow.

> ## Key specifications in practical context
> | Specification | What it means |
> | 153.36 x 77.76 x 165 mm build volume | A compact plate suited to miniatures and small parts. Batching capacity is far below a 10-inch Saturn-class printer. |
> | 7-inch 9K monochrome LCD; 8520 x 4320 | Elegoo lists 18-micron XY pixels. Fine pixel pitch helps detail, but exposure, optics, resin, supports, washing, and cure still shape the result. |
> | Tilting-vat release | Reduces the vertical peel motion required after each layer and supports faster cycles. The mechanism adds moving parts and clearance around the vat. |
> | Advertised maximum speed: 150 mm/h | A vendor ceiling, not a universal job rate. |
> | Automatic leveling and mechanical sensing | Reduces routine platform setup and can detect several incorrect conditions, but does not eliminate cleaning, inspection, or resin calibration. |
> | Camera, time-lapse, and claimed failure monitoring | Useful for viewing progress, though independent reviewers found the camera angle, dark cover, and failure recognition limited. |
> | USB and Wi-Fi | Supports local transfer and ChituManager network workflows. |
> | No built-in heater | Resin temperature must be controlled by the room or a separately qualified heating approach. |

> The **build platform attaches with a quick-release lever** and the machine
> calibrates the platform automatically before printing.

> [Alternativa mayor] **Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra:** the larger sibling makes more sense
> when a representative batch repeatedly overfills the Mars plate.

> [Alternativa de la misma familia] **Standard Elegoo Mars 5:** choose the lower-cost
> model when automatic leveling matters but the Ultra's fine pixel pitch, tilting
> vat, camera, and Wi-Fi are not worth the premium.
