# Artículo oficial de presentación de la Prusa CORE One (19 de noviembre de 2024)

## Extractos literales sobre rasgos visibles

### Cerramiento y estructura

> The CORE One is **a fully enclosed CoreXY 3D printer with active temperature
> control.** It's a machine focused on quality and speed, and it's extremely rugged.
> You can find the complete list of technical specifications on the product page,
> but let me give you a detailed insight first.

> The new design is based on **an exoskeleton structure,** often seen in nature
> (crustaceans, insects), where the outside skin serves as structural support for
> the body. In our case, it leads to both **improved printer robustness and great
> efficiency of manufacturing:** the same parts serve as the printer frame and also
> provide the final design. So unlike other printers, this isn't a welded cube with
> an enclosure around it or an open-frame printer in a box.

> For the frame, we're using **laser-cut steel profiles**, which is a really
> efficient, but also incredibly precise manufacturing method (with <0.1 mm
> tolerances). The same applies to the CoreXY gantry, which is a rectangle made from
> a single piece of solid steel – this makes it **incredibly sturdy and always
> perpendicular.**

> Plastic parts are made from tough **polycarbonate filled with carbon fiber.**

### Puerta

> And one more thing regarding the door (as well as all other transparent panels).
> They are made from a high-end polymer, so they are **virtually indestructible** –
> perfect for any kind of workplace and also super safe for transport. We didn't
> want to use glass because it's simply way too fragile.

> [Gracias a] the fans in the back, you can connect the printer to the air
> extraction system, **the door opens to 180°** so they don't stick out and won't
> block a more narrow space/corridor. **You can even flip the hinges and the door
> to the other side.**

### Ventilación / cámara

> The back of the printer hosts **dual fans for active chamber temperature
> control.** When you print with PLA or PETG, the fans ensure the temperature
> inside is as low as possible for maximum print quality.

> The only thing you need to do is to **open a tiny vent on top of the printer**,
> so the fans can draw colder air from the room.

> Thanks to the space-efficient body with **recesses on both sides**, the
> super-compact chamber can warm up faster and reach up to 55 °C.

### Bobina de filamento

> For instance, the spool is not in a hard-to-reach space (i.e., on the back).
> Instead, **it sits nicely on the side in a recess, so it's perfectly flush with
> the side of the printer**, keeping a small footprint. The other recess can be
> easily used for all sorts of holders, organizers, and other things you design.

> Since we're using proper metal almost everywhere, you can easily design all sorts
> of holders, organizers and shelves, and **attach them using magnets** to the side
> of the printer – the empty recess on the left side is the ideal space for such
> projects.

### Tamaño

> The overall space (w×d×h) you need for the CORE One is **30% smaller compared to
> the MK4S** and a whole **50% smaller compared to the MK4S in the Enclosure** while
> offering a **30% increase in print volume!**

Volumen de impresión citado en el mismo artículo: **250×220×270 mm**.

> [Respuesta de un comentarista en el propio hilo, con las dimensiones]
> 415×444×555 mm (X×Y×Z)

### Otros

> With the Prusa CORE One, we're also introducing **a new optional camera** [...]
> It will be available as a small module **to be placed inside the chamber** of the
> CORE One and connected via a USB-C cable to the mainboard.

> And more accessories are coming: we're finishing the development of **two types of
> filtration** (HEPA filter and advanced filtration system) **and a drybox.**

> [Sobre el MMU3] The unit is fully compatible with the Prusa CORE One and it will
> allow you to print beautiful, colorful objects with up to five filaments at the
> same time. [...] we are finalizing the way **it integrates to the printer's top
> section**.

## Notas del recolector

- El artículo es de noviembre de 2024 y describe la CORE One original. Prusa ha
  sacado después revisiones (CORE One+ / Gen 2) que comparten la misma plataforma;
  la página de producto actual del fabricante redirige a la revisión más nueva, así
  que este artículo es la fuente oficial disponible para el modelo pedido.
- Los rasgos más útiles y diferenciadores: **cerramiento total con paneles
  transparentes de polímero (no cristal)**, **exoesqueleto de acero**, **puerta que
  abre 180° y puede cambiarse de lado**, **dos ventiladores traseros**, **rejilla
  superior**, **bobina alojada en un hueco lateral a ras**.
- Original en inglés, no traducido.
