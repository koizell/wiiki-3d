# PLA in X1C - General discussions

**Eno** (Senior Member):
I myself go the other way when printing PLA as a warm chamber helps against curling and warping. I usually target 35 to 39°C chamber temp with a closed door and lid.

There are two important exceptions to this that I have found:

1. When printing with the Textured PEI plate (which requires higher bed temps), I need to open the door to prevent heat creep.
2. When the ambient temperature is high (summer), I need to open the door to keep the chamber from getting too hot.

Happy printing,
Eno

**Another user**:
One data point: my X1C is in my house (shoved into a closet whose door has been removed) and I always print PLA with the door closed and the glass top in place (with the AMS on top). Since the house is always kept at a comfortable-for-humans temperature, the chamber temperature rarely gets above 33ºC and I have not had any nozzle clogs.

**Another user**:
The TopProp is very stable and thoughtfully designed to prop your glass lid open for venting. Venting is recommended by Bambu Lab in many situations, for example when using PLA on the Textured PEI plate and Smooth PEI plate.

**Another user**:
Depends on the ambient temp in the room. My basement where the printer is, is usually at 18 Celsius. I leave the unit closed so drafts dont lift corners. If it was summer time and upstairs. Temps can hit 26C in the summer. So open it.

**Another user**:
It is true that ambient temperatures can contribute to clogs. I initially printed with the door and lid closed, but I achieve significantly better print quality with them open when printing PLA and PETG HF.

I print at high speeds and in good quality - during the AU summer here. Temps where printer is can go as high as 40ºC on some days. Doors closed, lid on, AMS on top. All my heat creep issues and extruder blocks ended when I installed an Obxidian hotend.

## Notas del recolector

- El hilo tiene varias respuestas de usuarios con experiencia imprimiendo PLA en la Bambu Lab X1C.
- La respuesta de Eno es especialmente útil: recomienda 35-39 °C de temperatura de cámara con puerta y tapa cerradas para PLA, pero menciona dos excepciones: cuando se usa la placa Textured PEI (que requiere temperaturas de cama más altas) y cuando la temperatura ambiente es alta.
- Varios usuarios mencionan que la temperatura ambiente es un factor clave: si la habitación está a 18 °C, se puede cerrar; si está a 26 °C o más, es mejor abrir.
