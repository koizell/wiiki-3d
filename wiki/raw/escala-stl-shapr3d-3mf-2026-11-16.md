### 3MF vs. STL

Both 3MF and STL are commonly used for 3D printing, but they differ in the information they can store.

-   **3MF:** Stores mesh geometry and supports additional information, including measurement units and colors.
-   **STL:** Stores polygonal mesh geometry without measurement units or color information.

Use 3MF when you need to preserve supported information such as colors and measurement units. Use STL for workflows that only require mesh geometry.
