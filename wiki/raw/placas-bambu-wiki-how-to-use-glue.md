# How to use Bambu Lab glue

Bambu Lab Glue

Glue Stick for Build Plate
Introduction: It provides suitable adhesion between the build plate and the model and is formaldehyde-free, making it suitable for printing filaments such as PC, PA, ASA-CF, PPA-CF, PPS-CF, PET-CF, PA6-GF. A regular glue stick can also be applied to the build plate to make it easier to remove models printed with certain high-adhesion filaments.

Liquid Glue for Build Plate
Introduction: It is an adhesive specially developed for 3D printing, suitable for printing filaments such as PLA, ABS, PETG, and TPU. When using Bambu Lab Liquid Glue, constant adhesion is maintained without worrying about models falling off or warping.

Notes
- Apply glue before you start printing.
- It is recommended to wait for the build plate to cool down before removing the printed model, which can reduce the risk of damage to the model and help the model to fall off the build plate more easily.
- If there is a lot of glue residue, it is recommended to clean the build plate before the next print to ensure good adhesion.

FAQs

What filaments does the glue work with?
Bambu Lab glue works with a variety of filaments, including PLA, ABS, and PETG. Before use, be sure to check the compatibility of the glue and choose the appropriate method for use according to the different types of build plates.

Bambu Engineering Plate: Before printing with any filament, it is recommended to apply glue on the Engineering Plate. Applying solid glue can make part removal easier, but since solid glue has a certain thickness and is difficult to apply evenly, the printing stability and first-layer quality are usually not as good as when using liquid glue. On the other hand, liquid glue may make part removal more difficult. You should choose the appropriate glue type based on the filament, printing environment, and other practical conditions.

Bambu Textured PEI Plate: When using this plate, it is generally not necessary to apply glue. However, when printing with filament that has a strong bonding to the plate, like PETG, the model may be difficult to remove. In this case, you can reduce its adhesion by applying the glue; at the same time, avoid damaging the model or causing the bottom to turn white when disassembling the model with force.

Bambu Smooth PEI Plate: It is recommended for printing high-temperature filaments. Except for printing PLA, glue must be applied before printing other filaments to increase the success rate and avoid damage to the plate when removing the model. For example, using this plate to print PA6-GF can significantly improve the printing effect and reduce warping.

Bambu Cool Plate (discontinued): This plate is only suitable for printing PLA, and glue should be applied before printing. When printing other filaments (such as PETG, ABS), bulging and sticking may occur.

Bambu High Temperature Plate (discontinued): Compared to the Cool Plate, the High Temperature Plate offers better heat resistance, is less prone to creep, and provides improved compatibility. When printing with filaments other than PLA, it is recommended to use glue to ensure proper adhesion between the filament and the plate, thereby reducing the risk of tearing the spare sheet for high temperature plate.

Bambu Cool Plate SuperTack: No glue required.

How to ensure the best adhesion?
- Before applying glue, make sure the surface of the build plate is clean, dry and free of grease.
- Apply glue evenly and apply appropriate pressure.

How dry should the liquid glue be before printing?
You can start printing once the coated surface has dried into a film and no longer forms strings.
The heatbed's preheating process at the start of a print is enough to fully dry the glue, so there is no need to spend extra time waiting for the glue to dry before printing. After applying the glue, you can place the build plate directly on the heatbed and start printing. By the time the heatbed reaches temperature, the glue will be essentially dry.

How long does the liquid glue take to dry?

| Temperature | Curing Time |
| Room temperature (24 ℃) | About 1 hour |
| Heatbed at 90 ℃ | About 45 seconds |
| Heatbed at 70 ℃ | About 2 minutes |

Does more thorough drying of the liquid glue result in better adhesion?
Not necessarily. The key to strong adhesion lies in how the liquid glue is applied, not in how thoroughly it cures. Tests show that a thick coat provides significantly stronger adhesion than a thin coat.

Application tips: Apply evenly and moderately thick. Four coats provide the best adhesion (beyond four coats, adhesion actually decreases).

How to clean Glue Stick?
Glue stick can be cleaned with water and soap. If the glue is dry, let the plate soak in water for about 30 seconds before washing it away.

How to clean up the residue of liquid glue?
Rinse with water and scrub with a non-woven cloth to remove it completely.

Storage conditions for glue?
Please store unused glue in a cool and dry place away from direct sunlight to extend its shelf life.
