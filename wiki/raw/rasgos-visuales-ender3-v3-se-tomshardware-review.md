# Creality Ender 3 V3 SE Review — Tom's Hardware

Publicado el 24 de septiembre de 2023. Veredicto del editor: Editor's Choice.

## Extractos literales

> ### Tom's Hardware Verdict
> Creality's latest Ender 3 is a beginner's dream with improvements previously
> thought of as luxuries while keeping the price extremely low.

> #### Reasons to buy
> - Affordable
> - Easy assembly
> - Excellent Auto bed leveling
> - Direct drive
> - Easy Interface
>
> #### Reasons to avoid
> - PC coated plate

> The Creality Ender 3 V3 SE is the latest incarnation of the budget friendly Ender 3
> printer. It's proof that even after making the new Core XY K1 it's flagship model,
> the company hasn't abandoned its beginner-friendly origins. This machine is a great
> choice for beginners because, unlike older Ender 3 models, the V3 SE is simple to
> build, has deluxe features, and is easy to use with **step-by-step instructions
> right on the screen**.
>
> Retailing at $199 and packed with features, the V3 SE is an incredible bargain
> that deserves a place among the best budget 3D printers and best 3D printers
> overall. A beginner's dream, It has borrowed points from other great Creality
> machines, with advanced auto leveling taken from the K1 series and **the S1's
> extremely capable Sprite direct drive**. The printer is faster than previous Enders
> at 250mm/s with the help of a **sturdy build and linear rods on the Y axis**. Its
> normal printing speed is around 150mm/s which allows the printer to use quieter
> fans.
