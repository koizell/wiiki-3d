# Sealed Box

**Introduction:** Filament absorbs moisture from the air when exposed for a long time. Printing with damp filament can cause various print quality issues. Therefore, in addition to drying filament before printing, **daily storage** also requires proper **sealing** and **moisture-proof measures**.

## ¶ Sealed Box

A **sealed box** is a common storage container for 3D printing filament. It provides a dry, airtight space that blocks moisture from the outside air, helping prevent filament from absorbing moisture and protecting print quality. In addition, for filaments that are not compatible with the AMS (such as TPU), a **sealed box** can be used for filament feeding during printing to keep the filament dry throughout the print.

- **Recommended Capacity:** 4 liters

- **Where to Buy:** Search for "*4L sealed box*" on online shopping platforms.

When using a sealed box for printing filaments like TPU, you need to install **rollers** inside the box (*Click to download model's STL file*). The rollers allow the spool to rotate smoothly during feeding and reduce feeding resistance. For details, see: TPU Printing Guide.

## ¶ Humidity Requirements for Filament Storage

-

Most filaments should be stored in an environment with **humidity below 20% RH**. The lower the humidity, the longer the filament stays dry.

-

**Dry vs. Normal Environment Comparison:**

- In a 20% RH dry environment, filaments can stay dry for about **2 to 7 days** (the exact time depends on how easily the filament absorbs moisture).

- The relative humidity of open air is usually 45% to 65% RH, and even higher in humid weather. In a typical indoor environment of around 55% RH, freshly dried filament will reabsorb moisture within just 2 to 12 hours — enough to noticeably reduce print quality.

## ¶ How to Use

- **Place a Thermo-Hygrometer:** For filaments that absorb moisture easily (such as PA, PA-CF, PAHT-CF, PA6-CF, PA12-CF, etc.), place a thermo-hygrometer inside the box to monitor the humidity. A reading below 20% RH is recommended.

- **Add Desiccant:** Place enough effective desiccant inside the sealed box to keep the interior dry.

We also recommend **checking the desiccant regularly** and replacing it when it is no longer effective.

For more information about desiccant, see: AMS Desiccant.
